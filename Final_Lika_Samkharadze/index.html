Why you should come to Georgia?


Georgia is one of the most beautiful countries in the world among the countries that are distinguished by their beautiful nature. Its area on this huge earth is only 69,700 sq/km. It has the most important location in the whole world. Georgia is located at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, which is a kind of bridge, connecting the East and the West. Despite its small size, Georgia has all kinds of landscapes, including deserts, coasts and mountains. The country is a paradise for hiking and camping lovers. You can also explore breathtaking lakes, canyons, waterfalls or relax at spa resorts.
